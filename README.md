One 1024x1024 image that sums up Wednesday 25 November 2026: forty dated world-news stories on a single page - twelve numbered cards plus twenty-eight briefs - spanning seven regions (Mideast 6, Europe 10, Africa 7, Oceania 1, Americas 8, Asia 7, World 1), with a day-in-numbers strip and an explicit date check that names the nearby stories deliberately left out because they happened on other days. Every story was reported on 25 November 2026 and is attributed to its source on the page. The shipped `entry.png` is 90,042 bytes; `main.py` recomputes its SHA-256 fingerprint, checks the exact size, the 1 MiB cap and the 1024x1024 IHDR dimensions, and only then copies the verified image out.
